Bitcoin OP_RETURN for Electrum Tool

Enter a Bitcoin address, an amount, and a memo (OP_RETURN). The result can be pasted into Electrum Wallet's "Pay to:" field.*

The transaction will include your memo as an OP_RETURN and will send the amount to the selected Bitcoin address. You can spend to yourself by using a Bitcoin address from your wallet.
