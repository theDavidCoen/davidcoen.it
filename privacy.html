Cookies — none, by design

This site is built as plain static HTML. I am proud to say it sets zero HTTP cookies. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no advertising network, no social-media embeds, and no third-party tracking scripts. Your visit is not profiled, and nothing on this site follows you around the web.

The only thing stored in your browser is an optional preference in localStorage (davidcoen_cookie_consent) so the notice below does not reappear after you dismiss it. That value stays on your device and is never transmitted to me or to anyone else.

  • HTTP cookies — none.
  • Analytics / marketing cookies — none.
  • Third-party trackers — none embedded on this site.
  • localStorage — one flag (dismissed) so the notice does not reappear; nothing else is stored.

There is nothing to opt into or out of: no optional cookies exist on this site. The banner is an informational notice only; clicking “Got it” records that you have read it.

To reset the banner, clear site data or local storage for this domain in your browser settings.
