Accept Bitcoin

Accept Bitcoin payments and keep bitcoin instead of converting to fiat. I run a free, non-custodial BTCPay Server instance for merchants and independent professionals worldwide.

Are you a merchant?

With this setup you can accept Bitcoin on-chain from customers worldwide. Funds arrive directly in your own wallet — I never hold your keys or your money.

How it works

  • A user-friendly Bitcoin checkout page for your customers.
  • Manage invoices, generate reports, and search payments easily.
  • Link your wallet (e.g. an xpub from Edge Wallet) so payments land in your account.
  • BTCPay Server is open-source Bitcoin infrastructure — not a money transmitter. I do not see, manage, or broadcast transactions on your behalf.
